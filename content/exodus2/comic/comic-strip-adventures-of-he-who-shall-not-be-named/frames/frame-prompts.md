# DALL-E / image prompts — 28 frames

**Series:** The Adventures of He Who Shall Not Be Named  
**Source story:** [comic-strip-adventures-of-he-who-shall-not-be-named.md](../comic-strip-adventures-of-he-who-shall-not-be-named.md)

## Global style (prepend to every prompt)

Clean line webcomic, soft flat colors, satirical forum-adventure tone, single horizontal panel ~3:2. Protagonist: empty silhouette or broken guest avatar; speech attributed with label **"User has been removed"** (never a real username). Readable dialog/captions in-panel: only proper nouns **OC ReMix** and **Second Exodus**; no other real forum names, game titles, or usernames. Background parody text may be blurred or nonsense. Dry humor, not reverent scripture.
